# RK System — Review Request Kit (ready to send)

Prepared 2026-10-09. Why: for "RK System water level controller Bengaluru"
the page-1 rivals hold their spots with hundreds of ratings, and the
listing pack already on file (LISTING_PACK.md) names rating count — not
the listing itself — as the ranking gap. Reviews are the one ranking
lever fully in the owner's hands. This kit makes asking take ~1 minute
per customer.

## What works TODAY (no waiting on Google or JustDial)

The website's own Reviews section is live and working:
- A customer rates via a personal link containing their booking ID —
  the site even says: "After a completed job you receive a link and
  your booking ID. Open the Reviews page, enter the ID and choose
  your stars."
- In the admin panel, each booking has an "ask" action that builds
  that link for you: it looks like
  https://rkwaterlevelcontroller.liveblog365.com/#review=<BOOKING-ID>
  (same link works on the github.io copy — bookings share one backend).
- Reviews appear publicly only after the admin approves them in the
  Reviews tab — so check the panel after sending a batch.

### Steps per customer (admin panel)
1. Open the completed booking → tap its ask/review action → copy link.
2. WhatsApp the customer Message A below with that link pasted in.
3. When the review lands, approve it in Admin → Reviews.
