Questions

How do I book?

Tap Book now, choose a new system or a service, fill in your details and share your location. Then send the booking on WhatsApp so it reaches us right away.

Why do you ask for my map location?

It gives our technician a Google Maps pin to your door. You can also paste a Maps link if you would rather not share live location.

What if my unit is not working?

Choose Service or repair when booking, or call 72046 38853. Describe the problem in the notes, for example which lights are on.

How do I rate your service?

After a completed job you receive a link and your booking ID. Open the Reviews page, enter the ID and choose your stars.
